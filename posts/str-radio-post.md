At Sharp Transportation, we're always looking for ways to keep our drivers connected, informed, and entertained while they're on the road. That's why we're proud to feature **STR Radio** – your dedicated station for everything Sharp!

STR Radio is designed with our drivers in mind. It's easy to tune in and provides a fantastic way to stay in the loop with local announcements, important updates, and even a little fun.

**What you'll hear on STR Radio:**

- **Local Announcements:** Get real-time updates relevant to your routes and operations.
- **Celebrations:** We love to celebrate our Sharp family! Tune in for birthday shout-outs and anniversary acknowledgements for our dedicated drivers and staff.
- **Easy Listening:** Enjoy a curated selection of music to make your long hauls more enjoyable.

**Got a song request? We'd love to hear it!**

Send your song requests to [str@sharptrucking.com](mailto:str@sharptrucking.com) with the subject line "Song Request". We'll do our best to get your favorite tunes on air!

STR Radio is just another way we strive to make your experience with Sharp Transportation the best it can be. Tune in and enjoy the ride!
